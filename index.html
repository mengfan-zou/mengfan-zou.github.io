02 · Research Practice

From research question to decision-relevant insight.

My approach combines social-science rigor with computational scale and a business-analysis mindset.

1

Frame

Clarify the user or information problem, identify evidence gaps and define what needs to be learned before choosing a method.

2

Design

Select data sources and methods that fit the question, balancing direct user evidence, qualitative depth and analytical scale.

3

Validate

Combine computational pattern discovery with contextual trace-back, iterative coding and human interpretation to protect meaning.

4

Synthesize

Turn fragmented evidence into structured themes, frameworks and next-step questions that support product and strategic decisions.

04 · About

A researcher moving from academic questions to applied insight problems.

Current positioning
“Research → evidence → insight → decision.”

Madrid-based · Mandarin native · Spanish C1 · English C1–C2

My background sits at the intersection of communication, digital behaviour, user research and computational analysis. During my PhD at the University of Alcalá, I studied how people interpret, participate in and shape interactive digital environments.

Across projects, I have designed primary research, collected longitudinal user data, worked with unstructured text and multimodal datasets, applied computational methods, and built human-validation steps to keep automated analysis grounded in context.

I am now translating that research discipline into User & Consumer Insights: understanding people clearly enough to support better product, experience and strategic decisions.
