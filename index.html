02 · Research Practice

From question to evidence to insight.

The portfolio spans primary research, structured product evaluation, qualitative interpretation and computational analysis. Method choice follows the question rather than the other way around.

1

Frame

Clarify the user, product or information problem; identify evidence gaps; define what needs to be learned.

2

Design

Select sources and methods that fit the question: survey, walkthrough, qualitative coding, multimodal analysis or computational methods.

3

Validate

Use model validation, contextual trace-back, iterative coding and human interpretation to protect meaning and reduce analytical error.

4

Synthesize

Turn fragmented evidence into themes, frameworks, experience patterns and evidence-backed recommendations.

04 · About

A portfolio built from research, not from résumé sections.

Current positioning
“Research → evidence → insight → decision.”

Madrid-based · Mandarin native · Spanish C1 · English C1

My background sits at the intersection of communication, digital behaviour, user research and computational analysis. During my PhD at the University of Alcalá, I studied how people interpret, participate in and shape interactive digital environments.

Across projects, I have designed primary research, collected longitudinal user data, worked with unstructured text and multimodal datasets, applied computational methods, and built human-validation steps to keep automated analysis grounded in context.

This portfolio brings those projects together as applied evidence: not only what I studied, but how I framed questions, chose methods and translated findings into useful insight.
